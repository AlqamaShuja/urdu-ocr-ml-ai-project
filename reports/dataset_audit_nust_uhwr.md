memorised text rather than from reading the image, so the count is reported
rather than silently removed.

## 9. Effect of Unicode normalization

| Form | Labels changed | Distinct chars after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NFC | 318 | 116 |
| NFD | 4967 | 110 |
| NFKC | 452 | 114 |

NFKC folds Arabic Presentation Forms into their constituent letters, which
is usually what an OCR evaluation wants, but it also changes other
characters. Whichever form is chosen, it is applied by the same shared code
to both models' predictions and to the ground truth, and the raw
transcription is kept alongside it in the manifests.
